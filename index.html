True beginnings

About Boost Painting

Phil started Boost Painting to prove a painter can be honest, careful and on time, every time.

Phil found painting while working for a high-end renovation company in Halton Hills. Of all the trades on a job, he always gravitated to the paint: a steady hand and patience came naturally. He went on to master his craft with a well-established painting company in Guelph, then set out to work directly with homeowners under his own name. Boost Painting (formerly P.A.C. Painting) was born in 2019.

Today the company offers a full range of interior and exterior painting, still built on honesty, transparency, good communication and an unwavering attention to detail.
