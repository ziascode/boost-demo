True beginnings

Our story

Phil set out to prove a painter can be honest, careful and on time.

Phil found painting while working for a high-end renovation company in Halton Hills. Of all the trades on a job, he always gravitated to the paint. The meticulous nature of it, a steady hand and patience, came naturally.

He went on to master his craft at a well-established painting company in Guelph, then started his own to work directly with homeowners under his own name. Today Boost Painting offers a full range of interior and exterior painting, without giving up the workmanship customers have come to expect.
