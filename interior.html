    Questions

    Before you book

    How much will it cost?

    Every home is different, so we quote after a free 15–30 minute visit. You’ll get a detailed, transparent quote, usually within 2–5 hours, covering prep, paint type and number of coats. The number you approve is the number you pay.

    Will my floors and furniture be safe?

    Yes. Paint goes exactly where you want it, without drips or splatters on your trim, floors or furniture. A job isn’t finished until the clean-up is.

    What does the warranty cover?

    Every residential project comes with our Standard 5-Year Warranty. If there are any issues, we address them immediately or at a time that suits you.

    When do you work?

    Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm. If your project needs a more flexible schedule, we’re happy to accommodate special situations.

    Which areas do you serve?

    Guelph, Kitchener, the GTA, Milton, Halton and Erin. We’re based in Rockwood.

    Are you insured?

    Yes. We’re WSIB certified and carry $2M general liability insurance.
